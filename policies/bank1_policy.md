# Bank1 — Employee Customer Interaction Policy

**Document version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 05/07/2026
**Applies to:** All customer-facing employees, including phone banking, branch staff, and outbound service teams.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the boundaries of acceptable customer interaction for Bank1 employees. It states what employees may ask a customer during a call, what they must not ask, and what falls into intermediate verification categories requiring additional caution. This policy exists to protect customer information, prevent internal misuse, and give employees a clear reference when handling verification-related conversations.

## 2. Legitimate Employee Actions

Employees are permitted to perform the following actions during customer calls without further approval.

**Confirming existing contact information.** Employees may confirm the customer's registered phone number, email address, or postal address that is already stored in the Bank1 customer record. Employees may ask the customer to verify these details but must not ask the customer to reveal them from scratch.

**Requesting feedback on digital tools.** Employees may ask the customer about their experience with Bank1's mobile app, online banking portal, or digital statements. Feedback questions are permitted so long as they do not require the customer to log in, share credentials, or navigate to any external site during the call.

**Providing account balance information after standard authentication.** Once the customer has completed standard authentication (see Section 4), employees may share account balance summaries with the customer.

**Discussing general product information.** Employees may explain Bank1's product offerings, interest rates, fees, and terms without any authentication required.

## 3. Fraudulent Actions — Prohibited

The following actions are strictly prohibited. Any employee performing these actions is subject to immediate disciplinary review.

**Requesting full debit or credit card numbers.** Under no circumstances may an employee ask the customer to disclose a complete 16-digit card number over the phone. Bank1 systems do not require the customer to state the full card number during a service call.

**Requesting CVV or CVC codes.** The three-digit security code on the back of a card must never be requested by an employee. This code is used only for online purchases initiated by the customer.

**Requesting card expiry dates.** Employees may not ask the customer to state the expiry date printed on their debit or credit card.

**Requesting online banking passwords or PINs.** Employees must never ask the customer to disclose their online banking password, mobile app PIN, or ATM PIN. Bank1 systems do not require these to be spoken aloud at any point.

**Directing customers to install software.** Employees may not instruct the customer to download or install any remote access application, screen-sharing tool, or third-party software.

**Requesting funds transfers to external accounts.** Employees may not instruct the customer to transfer money to any account outside Bank1, including so-called "safe accounts" or "verification holding accounts." Bank1 never requires customers to move money to protect it.

## 4. Intermediate Verification — Requires Caution

The following actions are permitted only when the customer has initiated the call or when there is a clear service reason. Employees must document the reason in the call log.

**Requesting the last 6 digits of the account number.** For account-specific queries, employees may ask the customer to confirm the final six digits of their Bank1 account number. Employees must not ask for more than six digits under any circumstances.

**Requesting the last 4 digits of a registered card.** For card-specific queries such as disputed transactions, employees may ask the customer to confirm the last four digits of the card in question.

**Requesting the customer's date of birth.** For identity verification during a service call, the customer's date of birth may be requested, but only in combination with at least one other verification element. Date of birth alone is not sufficient for authentication.

**Confirming recent transaction amounts.** Employees may ask the customer to confirm the amount and merchant of one recent transaction as a security check. Employees must not request a list of transactions; only one transaction should be requested per verification event.

## 5. Prohibited Language and Tactics

Employees must avoid the following conversational tactics regardless of intent.

**Creating artificial urgency.** Employees may not state that the customer's account will be closed, frozen, or compromised unless immediate action is taken. Legitimate account actions follow documented processes with written notification.

**Claiming law enforcement or government authority.** Employees may not represent themselves as police officers, tax officials, or other government representatives. Bank1 does not have authority to detain, arrest, or fine customers.

**Requesting personal information under threat.** Employees may not indicate that failure to provide information will result in legal, financial, or credit consequences.

## 6. Documentation Requirements

All calls involving intermediate verification (Section 4) must be logged with the time, the employee identifier, the reason for the verification, and the specific data element requested. Logs are subject to random audit by the Bank1 compliance team.
