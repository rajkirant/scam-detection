# Bank3 — Employee Customer Interaction Policy

**Document version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 05/07/2026
**Applies to:** All Bank3 employees engaged in customer-facing telephone communication, including customer support, retention, and outbound service teams.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the boundaries of acceptable employee conduct during customer telephone interactions at Bank3. Its purpose is to protect customer information, uphold regulatory obligations, and provide staff with clear guidance on what may and may not be requested during a call.

## 2. Legitimate Employee Actions

Employees are permitted to perform the following actions during customer calls.

**Requesting feedback on digital tools.** Employees may ask the customer about their experience using the Bank3 mobile app, online portal, chatbot, or digital statement system. Questions may cover ease of use, feature preferences, and general satisfaction. Employees may not ask the customer to log in to a service or share access credentials during the feedback conversation.

**Inviting participation in banking service surveys.** Employees may invite the customer to complete a survey about Bank3's services. Participation is voluntary. If the customer agrees, the survey is sent through official Bank3 channels — never a link stated verbally on the call.

**Providing information about product changes and offerings.** Employees may inform the customer about new Bank3 products, rate changes, promotional offers, and updated terms. This may be done without any customer authentication.

**Confirming appointment details.** For customers with scheduled branch appointments or advisor meetings, employees may confirm the date, time, and location of the appointment without requiring authentication.

**Confirming registered contact information.** After standard authentication, employees may verify that the customer's registered phone number, email address, and postal address are still current.

## 3. Fraudulent Actions — Prohibited

The following actions are strictly prohibited. Any employee performing these actions is subject to immediate disciplinary review and referral to Bank3's internal fraud team.

**Instructing customers to download software or remote access tools.** Employees may not direct the customer to download, install, or run any application on their device. This includes but is not limited to remote support software, screen-sharing applications, "security" applications, browser extensions, or any tool that grants access to the customer's device.

**Demanding installation of security applications.** Employees may not tell the customer that installing a specific application is required to protect their account, verify their identity, or continue receiving service. Bank3 does not require customers to install any application for account security purposes.

**Requesting funds transfers to external or "safe" accounts.** Employees may not instruct or request the customer to transfer money to any account, whether described as external, temporary, safe, or for investigation purposes. Bank3 never asks customers to move money to protect it.

**Requesting full account numbers, card numbers, or CVV codes.** Employees may not ask the customer to state their complete account number, complete card number, CVV, or card expiry date.

**Requesting online banking credentials.** Passwords, PINs, security question answers, and one-time passcodes must never be requested verbally by an employee.

**Directing customers to click links stated during the call.** Employees may not read out URLs and ask the customer to visit them during the call. Legitimate follow-up communication uses documented Bank3 channels only.

**Requesting one-time passcodes received by SMS or email.** Employees must never ask the customer to state a one-time passcode they have received. These codes are designed to be entered by the customer, not shared.

## 4. Intermediate Verification — Requires Caution

The following actions are permitted only for specific service reasons and must be documented in the call log.

**Requesting the last 6 digits of the customer's account number.** For account-related service queries, employees may ask the customer to confirm the final six digits of their Bank3 account number. Employees must not exceed six digits under any circumstances.

**Requesting the customer's registered phone number.** For identity verification, employees may ask the customer to state the phone number registered to their account. This must be combined with at least one other verification element.

**Confirming a recent transaction.** Employees may ask the customer to confirm one recent transaction (amount and merchant). A list of transactions must not be requested.

**Requesting the customer's date of birth.** This may be used as a supplementary verification element only. Date of birth alone is never sufficient for authentication.

## 5. Prohibited Language and Tactics

Bank3 employees must avoid the following conversational tactics regardless of the intent.

**Creating artificial urgency.** Employees may not state that immediate action is required to prevent account closure, fraud loss, credit damage, or legal consequences. Legitimate concerns are handled through documented procedures.

**Claiming external authority.** Employees may not represent themselves as police officers, tax authorities, immigration officials, or representatives of any government agency. Bank3 has no authority to act on behalf of these entities.

**Impersonating technical support from other organisations.** Employees may not claim to be calling from Microsoft, Apple, or any technology company. Bank3 provides its own technical support for its own systems only.

**Threatening consequences for ending the call.** Employees must permit the customer to end the call at any time, without conditions. Statements suggesting that hanging up will cause harm to the customer are strictly prohibited.

**Requesting personal information under duress.** Employees may not indicate that failure to provide information will result in adverse consequences, legal action, or reporting to authorities.

## 6. Callback Policy

If a customer expresses doubt about the legitimacy of a call, the employee must:

- Encourage the customer to end the call and dial the Bank3 official customer service number printed on the back of their debit or credit card
- Confirm that Bank3 encourages this practice
- Not attempt to persuade the customer to continue the current call instead

Discouraging callback verification is a serious policy violation.

## 7. Escalation

If during a call the customer describes a suspected scam attempt they have experienced from another party, the employee should:

- Take a description of the incident
- Advise the customer to contact their local consumer protection authority
- Log the report through Bank3's internal fraud reporting system for aggregate analysis

## 8. Documentation Requirements

All calls involving intermediate verification (Section 4) must be logged with a timestamp, employee identifier, the reason for the verification, and the specific data elements requested. Logs are audited monthly.
