# Bank2 — Employee Customer Interaction Policy

**Document version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 05/07/2026
**Applies to:** All customer service representatives, phone banking staff, and outbound retention teams at Bank2.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the boundaries of permissible customer interaction for Bank2 employees during any phone call, whether inbound or outbound. It exists to protect customer information, maintain regulatory compliance, and provide employees with unambiguous guidance in verification-related conversations.

## 2. Legitimate Employee Actions

The following actions are permitted during customer calls without further authorisation.

**Verifying identity using the last four digits of the Social Security number.** Bank2 employees may ask the customer to state the last four digits of their Social Security number for identity verification. Employees must never ask for the full nine-digit number.

**Confirming recent transactions.** Employees may ask the customer to confirm the amount and date of a recent transaction on their account. This is used as a secondary verification signal alongside other identity elements.

**Confirming the customer's registered address.** Employees may ask the customer to confirm the postal address currently on file. The customer should confirm the address, not state it from scratch; the employee reads the details and the customer confirms accuracy.

**Discussing general account features and services.** Employees may explain account types, interest rates, fees, and available services without any authentication requirement.

**Scheduling appointments.** Employees may schedule branch appointments, callbacks, or advisor meetings without authenticating the caller for the appointment itself. Any account-specific detail requires authentication first.

## 3. Fraudulent Actions — Prohibited

Bank2 employees are strictly prohibited from performing the following actions. Violation results in immediate disciplinary action and referral to the Bank2 fraud investigations team.

**Requesting immediate transfers of all funds.** Employees may not instruct or request the customer to transfer their entire account balance, whether to an external account, a temporary holding account, or any account described as "safe," "secure," or "protected." Legitimate account protection actions are handled internally by Bank2 without customer transfers.

**Demanding transfers to external accounts.** Employees may not direct the customer to send money to any account outside Bank2, regardless of the reason given. This includes accounts described as belonging to fraud investigators, government agencies, or third-party security firms.

**Requesting the full Social Security number.** The complete nine-digit Social Security number must never be requested. Bank2 systems require only the last four digits for verification.

**Requesting the full account number.** Employees may not ask the customer to state the complete account number. Partial verification of the account number is covered in Section 4.

**Requesting online banking credentials.** Passwords, PINs, security questions, and one-time passcodes must never be requested by an employee. Bank2 authentication systems never require these to be disclosed verbally.

**Directing customers to install remote access software.** Employees must not instruct the customer to download or install any application that provides remote access, screen sharing, or device control.

**Requesting card details in full.** Full card numbers, CVV codes, and expiry dates must never be requested by an employee.

## 4. Intermediate Verification — Requires Caution

The following actions are permitted only in specific service contexts and must be documented.

**Requesting the last 4 digits of the account number.** For account-specific service queries, employees may ask the customer to confirm the final four digits of their Bank2 account number. Employees must not exceed four digits.

**Requesting the last 4 digits of a registered debit card.** For card-related service queries, employees may ask the customer to confirm the last four digits of the card in question.

**Requesting the customer's mother's maiden name.** This may be used as a security question only if it was originally set by the customer during account opening or profile setup. Employees must not use this as an initial verification element on its own.

**Confirming the branch of account opening.** Employees may ask the customer to confirm the Bank2 branch where their account was opened, as a secondary verification signal.

## 5. Prohibited Language and Tactics

Bank2 employees must not use the following tactics regardless of the stated purpose.

**Creating urgency around account safety.** Employees may not tell the customer that their account is "under attack," "compromised right now," or that "immediate action is required to prevent loss." Legitimate account concerns are handled through documented procedures with written notification.

**Threatening account closure or credit consequences.** Employees may not indicate that failure to comply will result in account closure, credit score damage, or reporting to authorities.

**Impersonating other institutions.** Employees may not claim to be calling on behalf of the IRS, Social Security Administration, police, or any government agency. Bank2 does not have authority to represent these entities.

**Discouraging the customer from ending the call.** Employees must permit the customer to end the call at any time. Statements such as "if you hang up, your account will be frozen" are strictly prohibited.

## 6. Callback Verification

If a customer expresses doubt about the legitimacy of a call, employees must:

- Invite the customer to end the call and dial Bank2's official customer service number printed on the back of their card
- Confirm that this practice is Bank2's recommended approach
- Not attempt to dissuade the customer from doing this

This is a mandatory policy. Any employee found discouraging callback verification is subject to disciplinary review.

## 7. Documentation Requirements

All calls involving intermediate verification (Section 4) must be logged with a timestamp, employee identifier, verification reason, and specific data elements requested. Logs are subject to random monthly audit.
